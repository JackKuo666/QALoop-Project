THE CHALLENGE

Specialized knowledge needs a production process built around its sources.

Agricultural knowledge lives in research articles, expert questions, textbooks, wiki entries, patents, and germplasm reports. Each source presents a different structure and demands a different approach to question generation.

QALoop organizes these inputs into a shared workflow, connecting generation and quality checks with expert review and subsequent pipeline revision.

HOW IT WORKS

A feedback loop for data production.

From source materials to candidate QA, expert observations inform the next round of development.

01

Route & generate

Source-adaptive pipelines produce candidate QA using strategies suited to the structure of the input.

02

Organize & filter

Question-answer records use a shared format, with provenance and other metadata where available.

03

Review samples

Domain experts assess sampled outputs and provide scores, error labels, and comments.

04

Revise & evaluate

Developers use feedback to revise pipelines; a source-separated evaluation set supports downstream assessment.

↻

Expert feedback supports pipeline development and quality auditing across subsequent batches.
